# Email alerts (phase 2)

- 2026-11-26: Niko asked for email alerts: a reader ticks the companies they use and is emailed when one is compromised. The brief had ruled out registration; agreed as phase 2, built after Niko signs off the feed, with the database designed for it from the start.
- 2026-11-26: Mechanics agreed: no accounts or passwords. Enter an email, tick companies, confirm through an emailed link, manage or unsubscribe through a private link in every message.
- 2026-11-26: Sent through Resend, one email per incident, for confirmed or credibly reported events only. Never for unverified attacker claims. A daily digest may follow.
- 2026-11-26: The picker starts with about 150 widely used services, using Simple Icons logos where they exist and lettermarks otherwise, plus free text. Niko will supply logos the open set lacks.
